Features

Built for the way a hospital actually runs

Appointments that cannot clash

Requests wait for approval. A booking is refused when the doctor is busy, off that day, or the slot hits the break.

Live doctors calendar

Day, week and month views built from real appointments, with status, billing and notes in one click.

Complete patient records

Profiles, visit history, lab reports with file attachments, and a personal calendar for every patient.

Billing and receipts

Insurance and patient shares, paid and pending states, printable receipts and appointment slips.

Suppliers and stock

Track what each supplier delivers, see what is running low, and restock in a click.

Equipment upkeep

Medical and office devices with service dates, so nothing quietly goes out of maintenance.

Reports that add up

Revenue, purchases, completion rate, busiest doctors and top services, with CSV export.

Alerts and search

A notification bell for what needs attention, and one search box for patients, doctors, visits and suppliers.

How it works

From request to receipt

  1. 1

    Request

    A visit request arrives. Staff check the doctor's day and approve it, or reject it.

  2. 2

    Visit

    The calendar shows who is where. Status, notes and attached files stay with the appointment.

  3. 3

    Bill

    Insurance and patient shares are tracked, receipts are printed, and reports update by themselves.
