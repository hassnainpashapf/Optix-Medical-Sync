What the service is

Optix Medical Sync is hospital management software. A hospital or clinic can create a workspace, add its staff, and manage appointments, patients, billing, stock and reports. Plans, limits and prices are shown on the home page. Every plan starts with a free trial; when a trial ends the workspace becomes read-only until a plan is chosen, and nothing is deleted.
